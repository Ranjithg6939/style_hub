Our Milestones

The StyleHub Journey

From a boutique artisan studio in Mumbai to an internationally celebrated contemporary label.

2016

Atelier Founding

StyleHub was established as a bespoke tailoring studio, crafting limited-run capsule coats and dress shirts for discerning private clients.

2019

Sustainable Fabric Evolution

Transitioned 100% of our casual tees, denim, and fleece lines to eco-certified organic fibers and zero-waste dye processes.

2023

Flagship Showroom Launch

Opened our flagship store at 42 Luxury Avenue in Mumbai's Fashion District alongside our modern direct-to-consumer digital portal.

2026

The Contemporary Lookbook

Reimagining ready-to-wear essentials across India and beyond, championing accessible luxury and timeless aesthetic confidence.
